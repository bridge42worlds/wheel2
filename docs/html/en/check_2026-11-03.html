First full check of the rig

English translation of docs/check_2026-11-03.md — the Russian original is the source of truth.

Log of 2026-11-03, evening. Everything on: both wheels (six agents on the bus each and the coil agent), static accelerometer №8, the placebo wheel №22 and №23, phone №9, motor agent №10. The wheels were turned only slowly (PWM 30 %, ~25 rpm), not up to weightlessness (73 rpm) — the mechanics are still being looked at.

Firmware#

All 19 boards were flashed over Wi‑Fi with build 6da4bb9 (a commit of the same day), each confirmed the new version.

wheel 1: №3, №5, №6, №11, №12, №20, coil №13fcee6257 boards in 83 s
wheel 2: №14–№19, coil №214b221d0, 46f99607 boards in 79 s
static №8, placebo №22, №23fcee625, be44124-dirty3 boards in 75 s
motors №10fcee62575 s

New in 6da4bb9: own scattering ss (scatter.md), the alternating coil field and the field "with rotation" (sensors.md), the wheel top mark.

Coils#

DC field (off → "+" → off → "−", 4 times): ✓ on both wheels current flowed 4 of 4, the field by the wheel magnetometer 1305–1328 µT (wheel 1) and 1327–1342 µT (wheel 2), the sign matched the command, after switching off — background.

Counting and own scattering#

  • "Each on its own", 30 s, all 14 generators (both wheels and the placebo): ✓ 23–25 primes per second per board, 0 errors, all accepted the setting; heating over 30 s — 41–46 °C, the placebo boards without heatsinks — 58–61 °C.
  • **Own scattering ss** (№3 scatters itself, №5 next to it without, 40 s): ✓ 989 primes → 990 frames, 0 skipped, no waiting for noise, delay 0.61 ms; №5 heard 98 % of the frames; №3's speed did not drop (~25 primes/s, as №5).

Motors and rotation#

  • PWM 30 %, each wheel back and forth for 6 s: wheel 1 — 22–25 rpm, wheel 2 — 25–27 rpm; the rim |a| 0.87–1.12 g — as it should be at such speed (k ≈ 0.1).
  • Top mark: ✓ comes from №6, period 2601 ms = 23.1 rpm.
  • Found and fixed: the bridge swap (swap, "wheel 1 on bridge b", set on 30.10) had become wrong — "wheel 1" turned the wheel that carries №14 and №18. In the "by rpm" and "weightlessness" modes the server would spin one wheel while watching the rpm of the other (standing) one and raise PWM to the limiter. The swap is removed; checked: bridge a → wheel 1 (№6 up to 24.9 rpm), bridge b → wheel 2 (№14 up to 26.1 rpm), the other wheel stands.
  • Wheel 2 in the first pass hardly went forward (1.6 rpm in the last second). A repeat check — 6 starts of 6 clean: it moves off after 1.4–1.6 s forward and 1.8–2.0 s backward (with the motor ramp), runs at 25–27 rpm. Backward it moves off 0.3–0.4 s later — check for play.

Hiccups#

  • Right after flashing, the placebo wheel did not get two commands in a row (they are not in the agent's counter, no refusal); the third one arrived, after that everything worked. Outside a program the server does not repeat a lost setting — it hangs as "pending".

Not checked#

  • Both coils together — in phase and in antiphase (wheel 1 had been switched off by then).
  • The field "⟳ with rotation" on a coil — needs the wheel spun up, the next step.
  • Own scattering in a group (cascade, relay), the static sensor as a participant (not made yet).

At the end everything was stopped: motors standing, coils off, agents in "Stop".
