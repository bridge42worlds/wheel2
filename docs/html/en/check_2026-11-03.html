First full check of the rig

English translation of docs/check_2026-11-03.md — the Russian original is the source of truth.

Log of 2026-11-03, evening. Everything on: both wheels (six agents on the bus each and the coil agent), static accelerometer №8, the placebo wheel №22 and №23, phone №9, motor agent №10. The wheels were turned only slowly (PWM 30 %, ~25 rpm), not up to weightlessness (73 rpm) — the mechanics are still being looked at.

All 19 boards were flashed over Wi‑Fi with build 6da4bb9 (a commit of the same day), each confirmed the new version.

Counting and own scattering#

  • "Each on its own", 30 s, all 14 generators (both wheels and the placebo): ✓ 23–25 primes per second per board, 0 errors, all accepted the setting; heating over 30 s — 41–46 °C, the placebo boards without heatsinks — 58–61 °C.
  • **Own scattering ss** (№3 scatters itself, №5 next to it without, 40 s): ✓ 989 primes → 990 frames, 0 skipped, no waiting for noise, delay 0.61 ms; №5 heard 98 % of the frames; №3's speed did not drop (~25 primes/s, as №5).

Motors and rotation#

  • PWM 30 %, each wheel back and forth for 6 s: wheel 1 — 22–25 rpm, wheel 2 — 25–27 rpm; the rim |a| 0.87–1.12 g — as it should be at such speed (k ≈ 0.1).
  • Top mark: ✓ comes from №6, period 2601 ms = 23.1 rpm.
  • Found and fixed: the bridge swap (swap, "wheel 1 on bridge b", set on 30.10) had become wrong — "wheel 1" turned the wheel that carries №14 and №18. In the "by rpm" and "weightlessness" modes the server would spin one wheel while watching the rpm of the other (standing) one and raise PWM to the limiter. The swap is removed; checked: bridge a → wheel 1 (№6 up to 24.9 rpm), bridge b → wheel 2 (№14 up to 26.1 rpm), the other wheel stands.
  • Wheel 2 in the first pass hardly went forward (1.6 rpm in the last second). A repeat check — 6 starts of 6 clean: it moves off after 1.4–1.6 s forward and 1.8–2.0 s backward (with the motor ramp), runs at 25–27 rpm. Backward it moves off 0.3–0.4 s later — check for play.

Hiccups#

  • Right after flashing, the placebo wheel did not get two commands in a row (they are not in the agent's counter, no refusal); the third one arrived, after that everything worked. Outside a program the server does not repeat a lost setting — it hangs as "pending".

Not checked#

  • Both coils together — in phase and in antiphase (wheel 1 had been switched off by then).
  • The field "⟳ with rotation" on a coil — needs the wheel spun up, the next step.
  • Own scattering in a group (cascade, relay), the static sensor as a participant (not made yet).

Evening: weightlessness and the first runs#

Weightlessness (each wheel in turn, soft ramp 150 ‰/s): wheel 1 — 71–75 rpm, k 0.99–1.03, |f| at the top 0.01–0.06 g, PWM 72–74 %; wheel 2 — 70–75 rpm, k 0.96–1.01, |f| 0.02–0.09 g, PWM 69–73 %. On the move: the "+" field by the wheel magnetometer 1342–1417 µT; "⟳ with rotation ×1" — frequency by the top mark 1.18–1.24 Hz, phase error up to 5° (wheel 1) and up to 12° (wheel 2), field spread 1195–1391 µT. The PWM margin to the 75–80 % limit is small.

Found and fixed in the program:

  • Totals after silence. The server took an agent as "back" on any packet; an RNG agent after silence first uploads its prime log (hundreds of prm), and the totals were taken from old telemetry — the step's count came out ~0, and the next step was applied right away. Now the server waits for telemetry with counters (tel_t). The bug could have spoiled earlier programs with silence too.
  • Rate by the agent clock. The server divided the count by its own window (15.0 s), while the agents' counters spanned ~16.0 s and each start snapshot had its own age: ±10 % at random on a short run. Now the rate uses the agent's age.
  • Static does not spoil a run with static_spoil: false (searching for generation deviations while people walk around).
  • Wheels facing each other. The motors are mirrored: "towards each other" by the motors is one direction for an observer. The rig setting mirror (on): "towards each other" and "together" as seen by the observer. Checked at 30 %: the wheels turn in different directions, ~28 rpm each.

Five runs of 15 s (generation by two groups and K2 → K1 absorption; towards each other at weightlessness, field + −): after recounting by agent clocks — wheels +3.7 % (spread over agents ±3.9 %), the placebo wheel −9 % in the run with rotation and field against the control; absorption: K2 generation −3…−4 %. One control — no conclusion.

13 runs of 30 s (loop through the air: K2 counts only from the frames of K1's absorbers; 4 controls; field + −, − +, ∿ 1 and 5 Hz, ⟳ ×1; "towards each other", reversed, "together"): no anomalies (closest corrected p 0.027 at a threshold of 0.00006). The loop gives only ~2 primes per second for both wheels — per agent a count of ~10 per run, big spread. Most noticeable: candidates per second −24…−30 % with rotation (runs 3 and 6): the loop lives on frames between the wheels, and rotation changes the antenna positions. Note: "towards each other" in these runs was the same direction for an observer (before the mirror setting); real counter-rotation was the "together" run.

Static in the 13 runs: no anomalies. In all runs and controls — "⚠ check" from an outdated baseline (bands 64–256 Hz 11–13 dB quieter, the baseline line at 22.6 Hz gone) — the baseline was re-recorded. In the "reversed" run — a knock seen by №8 and the phone at once (kurtosis t ≈ +100): mechanics, apparently play when turning backward.

Next: the loop with "↺+" noise (RNG + reverse) and candidates as the main metric; 5–6 controls; check the mechanics when turning backward; runs with real counter-rotation.

24 runs of 20 s (three families, static searching)#

A fresh static baseline (2 min, everything standing): №8 — 23 / 22 µg/√Hz (ADXL355 datasheet — 25), phone — 59 / 51 (datasheet 70), Gaussian noise, even background. Families: C — standing, generation by two groups; A — towards each other (now as seen by the observer) at weightlessness, loop; B — the same reversed. Generation in A and B did not run — the "↺+" loop was given no absorber background, and every noise block waits for reverse noise too; static does not depend on it.

  • Standing, static is clean: "✓" in all runs except knocks (people walking); ∿ 17 Hz — №8 noise "non-Gaussian" (kurtosis 0.7–0.8), a single run.
  • With rotation — a line at 81.7–85.6 Hz on №8 in all runs and controls: the motor shaft, ≈ 68.8 × the wheel rotation. Added to the known lines (rotation from the rim, and when it is silent — from the motors).
  • Impulsive outliers on №8 ("impulsive interference"): in family A only at ∿ 5 Hz and ⊓ 1 Hz (kurtosis t +131 and +70, corrected p 0.003 and 0.01), but in family B also in the controls without field, together with knocks. More likely mechanics or motor brush sparking than the field; to check by alternating "off / ∿ 5 Hz".
  • "The Gaussian shrank" in the last run — outliers (kurtosis 19 on №8, 9 on the phone at the same time) stretch the SD and the scale, the core looks narrow on screen; the field was off — mechanics.
  • The phone — only knocks.

Results of the day#

  1. The rig works as a whole: 19 boards on one firmware, weightlessness holds (k ≈ 1, |f| at the top ≤ 0.09 g), DC and alternating field, "⟳ with rotation", own scattering, the program.
  2. No anomalies found. Everything noticeable is explained by ordinary things: radio between rotating wheels (the loop), mechanics and the motor (static: shaft line, knocks, outliers when turning backward), heating of the placebo boards without heatsinks.
  3. Fixed during the day: a wrong motor bridge swap; "towards each other" as seen by the observer (mirror); totals after silence; rate by the agent clock; static_spoil; the motor shaft line among known lines; mirror is now saved.
  4. Mechanics: knocking and outliers when turning backward, wheel 2 starts later backward; small PWM margin at weightlessness.
