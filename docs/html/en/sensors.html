Sensors, phone, motors and coils

English translation of docs/sensors.md — the Russian original is the source of truth. Labels inside the diagrams are in Russian.

The sensor code is shared, in the library firmware/libraries/agent_sensors. The wheel algorithm (rpm, weightlessness, curve shape) runs in the firmware (wheel.cpp) and line for line in Python (server/analysis.py), where it is tested on synthetic data (python tools/test_analysis.py); change it in both places. The static accelerometers compute nothing themselves: they send their axes and the server computes — static_watch.md. Wiring of the sensors, wheels, coils and motors — wiring.md.

All sensors are on the agent's I²C: D4 — SDA, D5 — SCL, power 3V3 and GND. At start the firmware itself looks for what is connected and reports in the telemetry the sensor's address, its ID (the ICM-42688 answers 0x47) and the modes read back from the registers, not the ones it meant to write.

Rim sensor: ICM-42688#

Searched for at addresses 0x68 and 0x69. Settings: ±16 g (at 2 rev/s at the bottom 8.25 g), ±2000 °/s (2 rev/s = 720 °/s), output 200 Hz, bandwidth 40 Hz, low-noise mode. It is read by its own low-priority task on the radio core — the compute core and the group protocol are not delayed.

What is computed per revolution (WheelTracker):

quantityhow
wheel axisthe direction of the smoothed gyro vector in the sensor axes: there is no need to know how the board lies in the tube
revolutionsthe angle around the axis, ∫ω dt / (1 + gyro scale correction); a full revolution is 2π
k = ω²r/gthe magnitude of the mean acceleration over a revolution: gravity averages to zero over a revolution, the centripetal part remains. k = 1 — weightlessness at the top point
|f| at the top and bottomthe smallest and largest over a revolution; at k = 1 — about 0 and about 2 g
radiusr = k·g/ω² from the measurement; the radius from the settings ("rotation sensor radius", 450 mm) — for comparison and while there is no measurement
curve shapek from the shape, gravity phase, gyro correction, second harmonic, mean along the axes — see "Calibration" below
wheel axes"towards the centre", "along the motion", "along the axis"; along the axis should be about zero, the rest is runout and vibration

Weightlessness at r = 450 mm is 44.6 rpm; the "neighbourhood" on the screen is k within ±5 % (43.5–45.7 rpm).

Packets — once per telemetry period, from one snapshot:

  • imu — revolutions since last time (up to 6), the current speed, the wheel axes, 20 readings in the wheel axes (20 Hz);
  • imr — the same 20 moments as raw sensor samples (acceleration and gyro), for recording;
  • imc — the curve shape of the same revolutions: k from the shape, the constant part of |f|², the gravity phase, the remaining gyro error, the scale correction, the second harmonic, the mean acceleration along the sensor axes, whether the revolution was even. The server matches it with the imu revolutions by agent time.

Rim sensor calibration#

The window «⊙ Калибровка колёс…» (wheel calibration) is in the ☰ menu (there is a button in «⟳ Моторы вручную…» too, and «📏 задать…» (set…) in the agent window). For each wheel it has three parts and the agent radii. Firmware without the imc packet — «◐ прошивка без формы кривой — обновить по воздуху» (firmware without curve shape — update over the air).

1. Curve shape and gyro scale. |f| over a revolution is not a sinusoid (at k = 1 it is a "tick" down to zero at the top), but its square is an exact sinusoid in the gravity angle φ in the sensor axes: |f|²/g² = 1 + k² + 2k·cos φ. Over a revolution |f|² is decomposed by the gyro angle (weighted by the angle covered, so acceleration within a revolution does not interfere):

  • k from the shape — half the first harmonic; the constant part must be 1 + k² (accelerometer scale). k from the shape and k from the mean do not have to agree: the accelerometer zero offset enters them differently.
  • Second harmonic relative to the first — about 0 for an even wheel, grows with runout, jerks, sensor offset: below 0.05 — «✓ форма чистая» (clean shape), up to 0.2 — «◐ колесо бьёт» (wheel wobbles), above — «✗ форма грязная» (dirty shape).
  • Gravity phase. Gravity makes exactly one turn per wheel revolution, so its shift from revolution to revolution, divided by 2π, is the gyro scale error. The tracker adjusts the scale by itself on even revolutions: speed equal to the previous ±0.5 %, gravity visible (k ≥ 0.05), second harmonic below 0.2. For an even revolution 0.2 of the error found is applied; the correction is no more than ±5 %. With the right correction the revolutions are correct and gravity averages to zero over a revolution (with a 1.5 % error about 15 mg would remain on average — the radius and zero offset could not be found). The window shows the correction and what remains — the median and spread over the last 30 even revolutions (at least 5 needed).

2. Radius and zero offset — from the slope. The mean acceleration over a revolution in the sensor axes is m = (ω²r/g)·"towards the centre" + b. For each axis — a line against ω²: the slopes of the three axes form the vector (r/g)·"towards the centre", its length × g is the radius; the intercepts are the zero offset b, mg; the scatter around the line is also in mg. Revolutions of the last 30 min are taken — even ones, with the adjustment converged (remainder below 0.2 %), at least 6, at 2–3 speeds spread at least ±15 % (for example 35 · 44.6 · 55 rpm). The window compares this radius with the radius measured with a ruler.

The switch «радиус для невесомости» (radius for weightlessness): «медиана оборотов» (median of revolutions) — the median of r = k·g/ω² of the last 5 revolutions (no older than 15 s; the zero offset enters the radius) or «по наклону — без смещения нуля» (from the slope — without zero offset) — the radius from the slope, the mean over the wheel's rim sensors. The motors take this radius in "weightlessness" mode; with no measurement — the radius from the settings.

3. Tachometer — an independent check: optical, on a reflective mark. The wheel runs at even revolutions, the tachometer points at the mark, the reading goes into the "tachometer" field → «записать точку» (record a point). The server pairs it with the mean rim rpm over the last 6 s — over whole revolutions, without the tachometer correction; «✓ ровно — можно записывать» (steady — you can record) when the revolutions of the window differ by no more than 1 % and the current speed is the same ±0.5 %. The correction is one factor through zero: c = Σ tachometer·rim / Σ rim² (tachometer = c · rim) and the scatter of the points around it, %. Up to 40 points, 1–300 rpm; best 3 at different speeds. «Применять: моторы и показ ×c» (apply: motors and display ×c) — the wheel rpm for the motors, the screen and the step record is multiplied by c, and the radius from the rim is divided by c² (it is computed from the gyro ω: r = k·g/ω²), so weightlessness stays where k = 1. Without points "apply" cannot be switched on; «очистить точки» (clear points) also switches off the correction.

Agent radii — with a ruler, from the axis to the chip of each agent of the wheel, 100–2000 mm («сохранить радиусы» (save radii) in the calibration window). Each agent has its own k_i = ω²r_i/g at the current wheel rpm (with the tachometer correction, if applied): in the calibration window, in the agent window («радиус … мм линейкой · k = …»), in the hover popup and in the record of every step (radius and k — from the mean rpm of the run; with the correction — also rpm_tach). k within ±1 % of 1 — «✓ невесомость наверху» (weightlessness at the top), otherwise "short" or "over". Weightlessness exactly at the top applies to the agent whose radius the rpm is held by; for the others — the "± from weightlessness" steps in the experiment (modes.md).

Where it is stored: tachometer points and switches — data/rim_cal.json; agent radii (with change history) — data/rig.json. API: GET /api/rimcal — the whole window; POST /api/rimcal — {wheel, tach} (a point), {wheel, del: i}, {wheel, clear}, {wheel, tach_on, fit_on}; POST /api/rig — {radius: {mac: mm}}.

Magnetic field: MLX90393#

Searched for at addresses 0x0C–0x13 and 0x18–0x1B. 20 measurements a second. The coil current is not known in advance, so the gain is chosen automatically: if a reading approaches the edge of the range — lower gain, too small — higher. The field is always in microtesla from the sensitivity table of the current gain (from 0.15 µT per count with a range of about ±5 mT to 0.75 µT with a range of about ±50 mT).

Inside the toroid winding the coil field is directed along the tube: at agent №20 in the wheel 1 tube — 1.20–1.26 mT at a current of about 0.9 A (coil_test.md). The Earth's field (about 50 µT) rotates in the agent's axes once per revolution. In the gaps of the winding the field is weaker and turns; how non-uniform it is inside the tube and how much leaks outside — the toroid field calculator (Russian).

Packet mag: the mean over the period along the axes, the magnitude (mean, smallest, largest), the gain, 20 individual measurements.

Static accelerometer (role S of the same firmware)#

An agent in role S only measures and sends the x, y, z axes 500 times a second; the spectrum, the noise baseline, alarms and the verdict "continue / repeat / check" are computed by the server — static_watch.md.

  • ICM-42688 motionless: 1000 Hz through the FIFO, averaged over 2.
  • ADXL355 (address 0x1D — ASEL to GND, or 0x53) — a precision accelerometer, noise per datasheet 25 µg/√Hz (the ICM-42688 has 65–70). If it is on the bus, role S measures with it: it is searched for before the ICM-42688, recognised by registers 0x00–0x02 = 0xAD, 0x1D, 0xED; bus 400 kHz. Range ±2 g; the sensor's output is directly 1000/avg Hz with its own filter (a quarter of the rate; avg 2 → 500 Hz); the sensor FIFO holds 32 triples with an x-axis marker and is collected every 10 ms; the axes are 20-bit, shifted into int16 in the packet (16000 per g, step 62.5 µg — below the noise). The registers are read back: FILTER 03, RANGE 01, POWER_CTL 00. The server recognises the sensor by counts per g (16000 / 8000 / 4000).
  • Measured on the same table: the ADXL355's own noise is 15–20 µg/√Hz against 61–80 for the ICM-42688, the accuracy of the mean over 1 s — 0.014–0.017 mg against 0.06–0.10; a single sample is the same for both — it is set by the table's vibration.

The phone (app phone/, version 1.1)#

The Android phone №9 is one more static detector. The app «Колесо-2 датчики» (Wheel-2 sensors; Kotlin, no third-party libraries) takes only what the rig needs:

  • uncalibrated accelerometer (the system does not adjust the zero offset; on a Galaxy A26 — ~500 Hz, ±8 g, step 0.0024 m/s² = 1/4096 g) — acc packets like a static agent's (int16, 4096 per g): on the server "live", the noise baseline and alarms work;
  • uncalibrated magnetometer, µT — mag packets with their own rate (hz), multiplier (div = 100) and step (step): «🧲 поле вживую» (field live) on the main screen;
  • background in tel (ph): mains 50 Hz in the field (Goertzel amplitude over 2 s — visible if the magnetometer is faster than 105 Hz), field oscillations over 2 s, Wi-Fi and cellular signal, battery (charge, temperature, whether charging).

Android does not give apps the raw radio spectrum — only the signal strength the radio modules hear. The phone has one screen: link, accelerometer and magnetometer (values, spread over 1 s, range over 10 s as x / y / z bars), background.

With the screen off. A foreground service (type health — sensors faster than 200 Hz) with an icon in the notification shade measures and sends: the CPU does not fall asleep (PARTIAL_WAKE_LOCK), Wi-Fi does not go into power saving (WifiLock). A service killed by the system comes back up, and after a phone reboot it starts by itself (unless «■ стоп» (stop) was pressed). At the bottom of the screen: «■ стоп / ▶ мерить» (stop / measure), «☾ экран гаснет сам / ☀ не гаснет / ◐ притушен» (the screen turns off by itself / stays on / dimmed — a lit screen means extra pickup) and, until it is allowed, «🔋 без ограничений» (no restrictions): without it Android in deep sleep may cut a motionless phone without a charger off the network. On Samsung also: Settings → Apps → «Колесо-2 датчики» → Battery → "Unrestricted". Screen off for 40 s (checked over USB): accelerometer 500 Hz, packets every second, no losses.

Link. Packets as lines — to POST /api/ingest on the server's port 8090, in batches 4 times a second; parsing is the same as for UDP. The phone first tries the USB cable (every 15 s the server runs adb reverse tcp:8090 if adb is present), otherwise Wi-Fi to the address from the server's beacon; if the address changes it takes the new one from the beacon. Field measurements carry the number of the first one and the time by the sensor's clock (i0, mt0, mt1, ms): what accumulated during a link break the server puts in its places, repeats are dropped; overflow of the field ring buffer — mlost in tel. For the server the phone is a role S agent with an address of the form 02:… (from ANDROID_ID), dev: "phone"; agent settings are not sent to it. The version is in fw (phone-1.1); when building, raise versionName and BuildInfo.VERSION together. Build and install:

cd phone; .\gradlew.bat assembleDebug C:\Android\Sdk\platform-tools\adb.exe install -r app\build\outputs\apk\debug\app-debug.apk

On the main screen#

  • The wheel column — from the rim sensor: the current rpm, how much is needed for weightlessness, «✓ невесомость наверху» / «◐ недобор, k …» / «◐ перебор, k …» (weightlessness at the top / short / over); in the picture — the rpm with the direction inside the wheel.
  • The agent window with a sensor: «∿ вживую» (live; accelerometer: oscillations, Gaussian, spectrum), «🧲 поле вживую» (field live; MLX90393 or the phone: |B| and axes, spectrum, field steps), «шум и точность датчика» (sensor noise and accuracy), radius by ruler and k.
  • Static sensors — the card under the picture: the verdict, live Gaussian, spectrum and oscillations of the selected sensor; in detail — the window «◆ статика подробно» (static_watch.md).

Testing without boards#

python tools/sim_agents.py [seconds] --port 5015 — a simulation in the same formats as the firmware: an agent with an ICM-42688 on a wheel r = 450 mm (rpm in steps 35 → 40 → 44.6 → 50 → 55 → 50 → 44.6 → 40 of 30 s each, the gyro is off by +1.2 %, the accelerometer has a zero offset of (12, −8, 15) mg — there is something to calibrate), an agent with an MLX90393, three static accelerometers with knocks, a line and a fault on a schedule; --gens N — more generators, --mot — the motor agent and coil agents. Run it with a separate server — the data and commands will not mix with the real ones:

python server/monitor.py --http 8091 --udp 5015 --data data/test/sim --no-beacon

Wheel motors (role M)#

Two brushed 36GP-555 motors (12 V, planetary gearbox 112 rpm, no encoder) turn the wheels; the motor agent №10 is a XIAO board in role M on the fixed frame, two DRV8871 bridges: wheel 1 — D0, D1, wheel 2 — D2, D3 (wiring — wiring.md). PWM 20 kHz, slow decay; speeding up and braking are smooth; the direction changes only through a stop and a 1 s pause; watchdog — no MOT command for 5 s, the motors stop smoothly. The server (server/motors.py) sends MOT a=‰ b=‰ r=ramp w=watchdog once a second.

The window «⟳ Моторы вручную…» (motors by hand): the wheels separately / together / towards each other / only 1 / only 2; wheel mode — standing / PWM / rpm / weightlessness (the rpm at which k = ω²r/g = 1; the radius — from the rim sensor, see "Calibration", with no measurement — from the settings); direction change by timer. In the "rpm" and "weightlessness" modes PWM = feed-forward + PI on the rim rpm (with the tachometer correction, if applied). «■ Всё стоп» (all stop) stops the motors too. To assign a board: agent window → «→ в моторы» (to motors). In an experiment rotation is set for every run (modes.md, program.md); at the end of a program the motors stop.

  • The rpm feedback is switched on only when the rim shows at least 60 % of the target: while speeding up and braking before a direction change the integrator does not accumulate error (overshoot on synthetic data 1–6 %); when it hits the PWM limit it does not accumulate either.
  • «✓ невесомость наверху» at the motors is by the measured k (per revolution; with the "from the slope" radius — by it), not by the radius from the settings.
  • The direction change timer ("in N min") counts from the start of motion: set for a standing wheel, it gives the first change after N minutes of running.
  • The motor and coil agents do not take part in telemetry "silence": the server sends commands only to those it hears, and a link break longer than 10 s would take them out of control.
  • "Forward ▶" is clockwise when looking at the shaft from the front; for a mirrored motor swap its OUT1 / OUT2 (wiring.md).

Wheel coils (role M, place "coil")#

A coil agent is the same firmware in role M, assigned to the "coil" place of its wheel (agent window → «катушка колеса 1 / 2», coil of wheel 1 / 2); wheel 1 — №13. A DRV8871 bridge from 5.1 V, D0 → IN1, D1 → IN2, the coil on OUT1 / OUT2 — wiring.md. The server (server/coils.py) sends MOT a=±level b=0 r=5000 w=5 once a second: the sign is the field direction, level 1000 is a constant current; a change of sign goes through a stop and a 1 s pause; no command for 5 s — the agent removes the current. A wheel may have several coil agents — the command goes to each one.

The field is set by the experiment's run (parameter "coil field", modes.md), by hand — the buttons "off / + / −" under the wheel on the diagram; at rest, at the end of a program, on «■ Всё стоп» and after a server restart — off. The coil agent does not belong to the wheel's groups, steps and subgroups: the server and the screen recognise it by its place, even when it is silent.

Field from the sensor. The wheel magnetometer sends the mean field once a second. Seconds when the coil is certainly off and the readings are steady give the background (the Earth, iron nearby), seconds with "+" — the direction of the coil field at the sensor. Readings minus background, projected onto "+", are the field with a sign: one can see whether there is a field, where it points and whether it matches the command; in the step record — coil_sense. If the sensor is moved, the background and direction are relearned within a few seconds.
