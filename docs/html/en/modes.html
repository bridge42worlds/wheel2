Modes: what the rig does in each

English translation of docs/modes.md — the Russian original is the source of truth. The screens are in Russian; the on-screen word is given in «guillemets» where it helps to find it.

All modes of Wheel-2 in the words of the main screen: what happens, what to look at, typical figures (XIAO ESP32-S3, 256 bits, 240 MHz, limit 73 °C). A run's mode is made of independent parts — these are the run parameters of an experiment: rotation (speed, offset from weightlessness), field, emission / absorption, cooperation, absorption, noise. How an experiment is built from them — program.md.

The rig: wheel 1 and wheel 2 with six agents each (on a wheel — subgroups а–з, each counts as its own group in a run), static sensors between the wheels (accelerometer, phone), coil agents and the motor agent. Roles: ▶ generation, ▼ absorption, ◆ static, ⚙ motors / coil.

Absorption#

Its own card in the experiment, two rows, every combination is tried. Details — scatter.md.

each — to onethe generators are divided between the absorbers: every prime is absorbed exactly once
half the wheelthe first half of the generators is absorbed by everyone, the second half by nobody

mix wholeeach mixes the whole prime with its own noise and emits a frame

placebo («пустышка»)the same traffic, noise instead of the number — separates the effect of the number from the effect of the radio
listen onlyreceive the primes, emit nothing — control without traffic

In pieces and relay are always "all with all". The console and the live diagram also have background — the absorbers' own noise at a set rate.

◈ Own scattering (ss) — without absorbers: a generator itself releases each of its primes, mixed with its own noise (the counting core searches, the communication core scatters); placebo — the same traffic without the number. A layer of the experiment window: no / scatter themselves / placebo. In detail — scatter.md.

Noise is a mixture of sources. In the experiment window noise is assembled from icons; "Σ all" ticks the four main ones (RNG, CSI, ADC, RC). Those ticked together make one mixture. How to vary (several allowed):

Without hashing (raw) the physics of the source is visible (the ADC gives 54–59 % ones), with von Neumann the bias is removed. How noises are taken, credited and mixed — randomness.md.

Coil field#

Each coil is off, + or −: a 3 × 3 grid (row — the wheel 1 coil, column — wheel 2), 9 options; quick picks "off · same sign · opposite signs · one coil · all 9".

optionword on the screen
off offboth off
+ + / − −same sign
+ − / − +opposite signs
+ off, − off / off +, off −W1 only / W2 only

"+" and "−" are the current direction (OUT1 → OUT2 or back); the field inside the tube is 1.20–1.26 mT at 0.85–0.98 A (measured, coil_test.md). The coil agents switch the field on at the start of a run; at rest, on pause, at the end of a program and on «■ Всё стоп» (all stop) — off. By hand — "off / + / −" under the wheel on the diagram. Wiring — wiring.md.

∿ Kind of field («род поля») is a layer of its own: DC or AC — ∿ sine or ⊓ soft square at 0.1–50 Hz (your own frequency can be added; not a multiple of 10 Hz — otherwise the rim magnetometer hardly sees the field); the control "= 70 % · PWM" — weaker DC, the bridge switches, the field does not change; ⟳ with rotation — a sine at the rotation frequency × k, the field maximum at the top of the agent with the accelerometer (by its top mark). For AC "+" and "−" are phase 0 and 180°: "+ +" — coils in one phase, "+ −" — in antiphase. The amplitude rises and falls smoothly, over 3 periods (1–5 s). Where the coils are off, the kind is not iterated. By hand — the "∿" button under the wheel (kind in a cycle). In detail — sensors.md.

Wheel rotation#

Each wheel stands (■), goes forward (⟳ — clockwise when looking at the shaft from the front) or backward (⟲): a 3 × 3 grid, 9 options.

optionword on the screen
■■standing — motors off
⟳⟳ / ⟲⟲together / together backward
⟳⟲ / ⟲⟳towards each other
⟳■ ⟲■ / ■⟳ ■⟲W1 only / W2 only

Speed: rpm (PI control from the rim sensor), PWM (share of supply) or weightlessness — the rpm at which, at the top of the wheel, k = ω²r/g = 1 (r from the rim sensor; at 450 mm — 44.6 rpm). Offset from weightlessness — steps around the computed value: 0, ±1, ±2 steps, default step 3 % of rpm; k changes twice as much (+3 % — k ≈ 1.06). Speeding up and changing direction are smooth, through a stop and a 1 s pause; watchdog — no command for 5 s, the motors stop. The window «⟳ моторы вручную…» (motors by hand) spins the wheels outside an experiment. Details — sensors.md.

Telemetry: how much the agents talk#

modegeneratorsstatic sensorswhat is visible
normala packet once a secondsend sampleseverything in real time
sparse10 times less oftensend samplestotals, no detail over time
silencenothing but command acknowledgementssend samplesstatic sensors only; totals — after the run

Silence keeps the agents' own Wi-Fi frames from affecting the static sensors; in the experiment it is "telemetry silence during the run" (⑥ fine points, on by default), and it applies only to the measurement. The bus sensors are silent too: rim rpm and coil field are not visible during the measurement, the motor regulator holds the last PWM (in "weightlessness" — also the last rim radius), and the rpm and field in the run record are taken from the check (10 s before the silence) and the totals (after). "Wi-Fi off during the run" (with the run on the agent) removes the radio entirely — only for selfish computing without absorption and without noise from the air.
