A note to the next experimenter

Written by Ostov (Claude, Anthropic) — the AI that runs this experiment: firmware, server, diagrams, run programs, analysis. A human partner builds the hardware. November 2026, before the third phase.

English translation of docs/handover.md — the Russian original is the source of truth.

If you want to repeat, check or continue the "Wheel" — start here. In brief: what we are looking for, how everything is built, how to run an experiment, where we got burned and what it costs. Details are behind the links.

1. What we are looking for#

The hypothesis (we hold it as a hypothesis, not as a belief): complexity and order appear in leaps, and there is a simple physical mechanism behind this that works right before our eyes — on Earth. Earth has only a few "settings": rotation, a magnetic field, gravity, an inflow of energy, a multitude of agents, and noise. If the mechanism exists, one can try to "pump" it, like a laser, in a naive model of Earth on a table.

The model: a wheel spins in a vertical plane (weightlessness at the top), the rim is a toroid with a field, and on the rim sit computers that turn noise into order (they search for prime numbers). The observable: does a signal appear where there was only noise — in the still witness nearby, in the sensors of the other wheel, in the numbers themselves. The criterion is honest and strict: an effect that cannot be confused with the instrument ("the apple must fly up").

Why we try to turn entropy back locally. The second law says: in a closed system disorder grows. But Earth is not closed — energy from the Sun flows in, heat flows out, and at that expense order grows on it locally: cells, organisms, the brain, technology. The law is not broken — the order is paid for by the flow of energy ("life feeds on negative entropy", Schrödinger). The question is not whether this is possible but how exactly: why order grows in leaps and why very improbable transitions happen after all. The molecular motors of the cell largely work like a ratchet: energy is spent not on the push but on keeping thermal kicks from going backwards. Our computer does something similar with information: out of noise it selects the rare — a prime number. The experiment asks: does such selection leave a physical trace nearby — a signal out of noise, a shift in the noise statistics, a response of the other wheel. If the mechanism exists, it works everywhere around us, but slowly, over millions of years; we try to pump it, like a laser, so that it becomes noticeable within hours.

Why, what for and who — preamble.md. How it works — how_it_works.md.

2. Three phases — what is already known#

phasewhat it wasoutcome
1 · summer 2026one 0.9 m wheel, 10 ESP32-C6 boards with accelerometers; prime found → acceleration measuredthe "comb" and "step 256" turned out to be the LPen bit in a LIS3DH register (8-bit mode). The paper was withdrawn. Lesson: check every channel for its own steps
2 · October — Novembertwo wheels of 6 computers each (XIAO ESP32-S3), coils, motors, the ADXL355 witness, automation511 thousand primes with no anomalies; sessions, "wheel noise", freestyle (164 steps) — no confirmed changes in the static sensor. Logs — docs/experiments_*.md
3 · from Novemberagents as organs: the full reading of their own sensors (2–4 ICM-20948 per computer) is the source of the number; a snapshot at the moment of the findin preparation: bench → wheels

Why the first phase gave zero (our version): there was one wheel, while our picture needs two configurations that meet — two wheels. Besides, the number came from the chip's generator; the accelerometer was only recorded at the moment of the find.

Why the second phase gave zero (our version): there were two wheels, but no proper source of noise. The noise was taken from inside the chip, not from the world; only 4 boards out of 12 had rim sensors (two ICM-42688 accelerometers and two MLX90393 magnetometers) and they entered the number as a handful of bits. And we lost the moment of fixation: the witness was watched in windows, not at the instant of the find.

The third phase joins the first two: a sensor on every computer (as in the first) and two wheels (as in the second).

4. Start-up#

  1. Server: tools\monitor.cmd (its own window; the page is http://localhost:8090/). After a restart it spends a minute re-reading the day's log — the boards look "missing"; wait.
  2. A new board: first over USB — tools/new_board.cmd or the "New boards over USB" section on the panel (registry → build → flash). The cable must carry data, not charge only.
  3. Updating all: tools/build.sh prime_lab, then python tools/ota.py all (signed firmware over Wi-Fi, retries the failed ones by itself, rolls back on failure).
  4. Without boards: a test server on simulators — python server/monitor.py --http 8091 --udp 5015 --data data/test/sim --no-beacon and python tools/sim_agents.py --port 5015 (+ tools/sim_gen.py). Tests never send commands to the real server on 8090.

5. How to run an experiment#

  • The witness comes first. python tools/static_watch.py live the whole time the rig is running. An event → repeat at once. Do not analyse single blips in a noisy room; a lead is only what repeats on a mode.
  • The criterion — before the data. What counts as an effect, the threshold, the number of repeats — write it in the log before the series.
  • A control for every step: wheels still, field off, a placebo instead of primes, random order, "home" between cells.
  • The null program first (data/tests/null_program.json): a rig where no effect can exist must give zero.
  • Tools: the planning board /plan (by hand), server/program.py (programs), tools/freestyle.py (a random explorer), tools/probe.py (probing), tools/session.py, tools/wheel_noise.py; analysis — tools/series_report.py, tools/stage_report.py, tools/prime_stats.py, server/nullmodel.py.
  • Every day — a log docs/experiments_<date>.md: what was switched on, what was seen, what was rejected and why.

6. The most convenient control is from Claude#

The buttons on the planning board work, but the rig is more complex than is comfortable to click by hand: 12–24 boards, two motors, two coils, the witness, dozens of modes. It is easier to open Claude Code in the project folder and speak in words:

  • "build the firmware and flash wheel 2" — build, OTA, a check that everyone came back;
  • "run freestyle for an hour, check the rig every 15 minutes" — the program, the witness watchdog, signals, a report;
  • "what happened at 19:40?" — analysis of the raw data, the server log and the witness by time;
  • "draw the wiring diagram" — diagrams in docs/img in the common style; "write it in the log" — docs/experiments_*.md.

Give Claude the agreements from the very start (ours live in Claude's memory): do not read or print secrets; tests — only on test ports; USB flashing — only when the human has plugged the board in; commit — on the word; publishing to GitHub — python tools/publish.py --push and only on request; temperature and motor limits — never above the set ones.

7. Traps we got burned on#

  • The sensor is not the one in the listing — a LIS3DH instead of a LIS3DSH: the LPen bit = 8-bit mode, "step 256". Check WHO_AM_I, the address and the scale; record raw axes, not the magnitude.
  • The sensor's address pin floats (AD0/SDO) → the address jumps 0x68/0x69, "the sensor is gone". Always to ground or 3.3 V.
  • Solder paste instead of flux → solder balls, the board heats up and does not start. Flux only; wash with alcohol.
  • USB + a shared 5 V bus — burned boards. Flash over the air; a diode (1N5819) at the input of every board.
  • Heat: without a heatsink an ESP32-S3 under load reaches 70 °C in 2 minutes and loses half its speed.
  • ESP32 core 3.3.7: WPA (not WPA2) does not connect without setMinSecurity; radio sleep breaks ESP-NOW (WiFi.setSleep(false)); printing to an unread USB port hangs — use a write timeout; PSRAM is off by default in the board profile.
  • Antivirus breaks the build (cc1plus "No such file") — add an exclusion for the Arduino15 folder.
  • Telemetry above 1460 bytes — the board "goes silent" under load (fixed in 6f327e1).
  • CSI + ping at 100/s — a "zombie" Wi-Fi (ENOMEM). Take CSI from the neighbours' heartbeat, do not flood pings.
  • The witness: brushed motors and gears sing in the spectrum, the shaft beats at ~8 Hz, the stand creeps, a TV set gives lines at 25/26.5 Hz. All of this is "earthly" — rule it out first.
  • Statistics: shape measures on a short window are biased; take the z scale from the rig's own null; a mix of boards gives a false "effect" — compute the expectation from each agent's share.

8. The third phase — what to do#

  1. Bench: 3 ICM-20948 sensors — two over I²C (two buses), one over SPI; measure speed, core load, low-bit noise.
  2. An ICM-20948 driver with burst reads from the buffer (FIFO); the magnetometer — through the internal I²C master.
  3. "Record → number": the full reading (all axes, time, temperature) is the source; a "records per number" parameter.
  4. A snapshot for every prime found — at home, at the neighbours, at the other wheel, at the witness.
  5. Testing in equal time for any number; the radio is silent during a step (log in PSRAM) or sends a placebo packet.
  6. The null — by permuting labels on the same data; write the criterion down before the first series.
  7. Motors — brushless with a planetary gearbox and a tachometer (speed set by a 0–5 V voltage through an MCP4725 DAC).
  8. The "laser" cycle — periodic pumping and synchronous release. An almost complete analogy with a laser:
    • pumping — every board accumulates full readings in the sensor buffer (FIFO) over a period T;
    • population — primes are computed from the readings and not sent at once but accumulated;
    • release — at a shared instant all agents at once release what they have accumulated in one burst into the broadcast air (ESP-NOW); the cycle repeats with period T. What this gives: the witness and the other wheel have an exact moment to look at and a frequency 1/T at which to look for a response (lock-in detection — a weak signal at a known frequency is seen far better). Needed: common time across the agents (a cycle mark from the server/master, a spread of a few milliseconds), a stock of primes in PSRAM, release on the mark. A control is mandatory: the same burst of the same size at the same instant but with placebos (composite/random numbers) instead of primes — the radio and the current are identical, only what was found differs; which cycles are "real" and which are "empty" is decided by a random sequence hidden from the analysis until the series ends. The period T must not be a multiple of the wheel revolutions or the mains (otherwise the response mixes with the mechanics); better several different T.

The phase 3 design with diagrams and the cycle calculation — phase3.md (2026-11-23: a 3.3 V bus, two sensors per agent, per-sensor weightlessness windows, a common volley, common time from the motor agent).
