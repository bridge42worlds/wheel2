# First full check of the rig

*English translation of [docs/check_2026-11-03.md](../check_2026-11-03.md) — the Russian original is the source of truth.*

Log of 2026-11-03, evening. Everything on: both wheels (six agents on the bus each and the coil agent), static
accelerometer №8, the placebo wheel №22 and №23, phone №9, motor agent №10. The wheels were turned only slowly
(PWM 30 %, ~25 rpm), not up to weightlessness (73 rpm) — the mechanics are still being looked at.

## Counting and own scattering

* **"Each on its own", 30 s, all 14 generators** (both wheels and the placebo): ✓ 23–25 primes per second per board,
  0 errors, all accepted the setting; heating over 30 s — 41–46 °C, the placebo boards without heatsinks — 58–61 °C.
* **Own scattering `ss`** (№3 scatters itself, №5 next to it without, 40 s): ✓ 989 primes → 990 frames, 0 skipped, no
  waiting for noise, delay 0.61 ms; №5 heard 98 % of the frames; №3's speed did not drop (~25 primes/s, as №5).

## Motors and rotation

* PWM 30 %, each wheel back and forth for 6 s: wheel 1 — 22–25 rpm, wheel 2 — 25–27 rpm; the rim \|a\| 0.87–1.12 g — as
  it should be at such speed (k ≈ 0.1).
* Top mark: ✓ comes from №6, period 2601 ms = 23.1 rpm.
* **Found and fixed:** the bridge swap (`swap`, "wheel 1 on bridge b", set on 30.10) had become wrong — "wheel 1" turned
  the wheel that carries №14 and №18. In the "by rpm" and "weightlessness" modes the server would spin one wheel while
  watching the rpm of the other (standing) one and raise PWM to the limiter. The swap is removed; checked: bridge a →
  wheel 1 (№6 up to 24.9 rpm), bridge b → wheel 2 (№14 up to 26.1 rpm), the other wheel stands.
* Wheel 2 in the first pass hardly went forward (1.6 rpm in the last second). A repeat check — 6 starts of 6 clean: it
  moves off after 1.4–1.6 s forward and 1.8–2.0 s backward (with the motor ramp), runs at 25–27 rpm. Backward it moves
  off 0.3–0.4 s later — check for play.

## Hiccups

* Right after flashing, the placebo wheel did not get two commands in a row (they are not in the agent's counter, no
  refusal); the third one arrived, after that everything worked. Outside a program the server does not repeat a lost
  setting — it hangs as "pending".
