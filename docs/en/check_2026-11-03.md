## Evening: weightlessness and the first runs

**Weightlessness** (each wheel in turn, soft ramp 150 ‰/s): wheel 1 — 71–75 rpm, k 0.99–1.03, |f| at the top 0.01–0.06 g,
PWM 72–74 %; wheel 2 — 70–75 rpm, k 0.96–1.01, |f| 0.02–0.09 g, PWM 69–73 %. On the move: the "+" field by the wheel
magnetometer 1342–1417 µT; "⟳ with rotation ×1" — frequency by the top mark 1.18–1.24 Hz, phase error up to 5° (wheel 1)
and up to 12° (wheel 2), field spread 1195–1391 µT. The PWM margin to the 75–80 % limit is small.

**Found and fixed in the program:**

* **Totals after silence.** The server took an agent as "back" on any packet; an RNG agent after silence first uploads
  its prime log (hundreds of prm), and the totals were taken from old telemetry — the step's count came out ~0, and the
  next step was applied right away. Now the server waits for telemetry with counters (`tel_t`). The bug could have
  spoiled earlier programs with silence too.
* **Rate by the agent clock.** The server divided the count by its own window (15.0 s), while the agents' counters
  spanned ~16.0 s and each start snapshot had its own age: ±10 % at random on a short run. Now the rate uses the agent's `age`.
* **Static does not spoil a run** with `static_spoil: false` (searching for generation deviations while people walk around).
* **Wheels facing each other.** The motors are mirrored: "towards each other" by the motors is one direction for an
  observer. The rig setting `mirror` (on): "towards each other" and "together" as seen by the observer. Checked at 30 %:
  the wheels turn in different directions, ~28 rpm each.

**Five runs of 15 s** (generation by two groups and K2 → K1 absorption; towards each other at weightlessness, field + −):
after recounting by agent clocks — wheels +3.7 % (spread over agents ±3.9 %), the placebo wheel −9 % in the run with
rotation and field against the control; absorption: K2 generation −3…−4 %. One control — no conclusion.

**13 runs of 30 s** (loop through the air: K2 counts only from the frames of K1's absorbers; 4 controls; field + −, − +,
∿ 1 and 5 Hz, ⟳ ×1; "towards each other", reversed, "together"): no anomalies (closest corrected p 0.027 at a threshold of
0.00006). The loop gives only ~2 primes per second for both wheels — per agent a count of ~10 per run, big spread. Most
noticeable: candidates per second −24…−30 % with rotation (runs 3 and 6): the loop lives on frames between the wheels,
and rotation changes the antenna positions. Note: "towards each other" in these runs was the same direction for an
observer (before the `mirror` setting); real counter-rotation was the "together" run.

**Static in the 13 runs:** no anomalies. In all runs and controls — "⚠ check" from an outdated baseline (bands 64–256 Hz
11–13 dB quieter, the baseline line at 22.6 Hz gone) — the baseline was re-recorded. In the "reversed" run — a knock seen
by №8 and the phone at once (kurtosis t ≈ +100): mechanics, apparently play when turning backward.

**Next:** the loop with "↺+" noise (RNG + reverse) and candidates as the main metric; 5–6 controls; check the mechanics
when turning backward; runs with real counter-rotation.

## 24 runs of 20 s (three families, static searching)

A fresh static baseline (2 min, everything standing): №8 — 23 / 22 µg/√Hz (ADXL355 datasheet — 25), phone — 59 / 51
(datasheet 70), Gaussian noise, even background. Families: C — standing, generation by two groups; A — towards each other
(now as seen by the observer) at weightlessness, loop; B — the same reversed. **Generation in A and B did not run** — the
"↺+" loop was given no absorber background, and every noise block waits for reverse noise too; static does not depend on it.

* Standing, static is clean: "✓" in all runs except knocks (people walking); ∿ 17 Hz — №8 noise "non-Gaussian"
  (kurtosis 0.7–0.8), a single run.
* With rotation — a line at 81.7–85.6 Hz on №8 in all runs and controls: the motor shaft, ≈ 68.8 × the wheel rotation.
  Added to the known lines (rotation from the rim, and when it is silent — from the motors).
* Impulsive outliers on №8 ("impulsive interference"): in family A only at ∿ 5 Hz and ⊓ 1 Hz (kurtosis t +131 and +70,
  corrected p 0.003 and 0.01), but in family B also in the controls without field, together with knocks. More likely
  mechanics or motor brush sparking than the field; to check by alternating "off / ∿ 5 Hz".
* "The Gaussian shrank" in the last run — outliers (kurtosis 19 on №8, 9 on the phone at the same time) stretch the SD
  and the scale, the core looks narrow on screen; the field was off — mechanics.
* The phone — only knocks.

## Results of the day

1. The rig works as a whole: 19 boards on one firmware, weightlessness holds (k ≈ 1, |f| at the top ≤ 0.09 g), DC and
   alternating field, "⟳ with rotation", own scattering, the program.
2. No anomalies found. Everything noticeable is explained by ordinary things: radio between rotating wheels (the loop),
   mechanics and the motor (static: shaft line, knocks, outliers when turning backward), heating of the placebo boards
   without heatsinks.
3. Fixed during the day: a wrong motor bridge swap; "towards each other" as seen by the observer (`mirror`); totals after
   silence; rate by the agent clock; `static_spoil`; the motor shaft line among known lines; `mirror` is now saved.
4. Mechanics: knocking and outliers when turning backward, wheel 2 starts later backward; small PWM margin at weightlessness.
